Stacking linear layers doesn't add expressive power: each layer is just a matrix multiplication, and the product of any number of matrices is itself one matrix. Five linear layers with no activation between them compute exactly the same family of functions as a single linear layer - they can only ever draw a straight decision boundary.

That's exactly what happens on the rings above: the 1-layer and 5-linear-layer networks land on the same accuracy and the same straight-line boundary, no matter how long they train. Insert a ReLU between each of those same five layers, though, and the composition is no longer linear - the network can bend its boundary around the inner ring and solve the task.

Depth lets you think for longer. Nonlinearity lets you think differently. Real intelligence needs both.

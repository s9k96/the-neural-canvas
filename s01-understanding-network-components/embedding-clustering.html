Embeddings Projected to 2D (PCA)

Proof: Nearest Neighbor by Category

For each token, the closest other token in the full 8D embedding space — never told categories exist, only trained to predict what comes next.
Train to compute nearest neighbors.

Claim: a model trained only to predict the next token in a tiny synthetic grammar will cluster related tokens in embedding space, even though "similarity" was never part of the loss — cross-entropy on the next token is the only signal it ever sees.

Build: sentences are generated from animal verb noun END, where the verb following an animal is unconstrained and the noun following a verb is unconstrained. That means every animal shares the same "what comes next" distribution (a verb), every verb shares the same "what comes next" distribution (any noun), and fruits — which never appear as a sentence's subject — get a distribution all their own. The model is a single embedding table feeding directly into a softmax: nothing in its architecture knows about categories.

We are known by the company we keep.

The World, Viewed From India

Designing Bharat-40B — a Gemma-4-class, 40B dense model strong in coding, agentic work, and 11 Indian scripts. This is the data, cleaning, tokenizer, and evaluation blueprint, with every number reasoned to a decision.

"India-first" is not a marketing layer on a Western model. It is three engineering commitments that shape every decision below.

Knowledge distribution

Factual defaults come from Indian sources (Constitution & BNS, NCERT, Indian-authored history, lakh/crore, festivals). The Indian worldview is pre-trained in, not prompted.

Fertility parity = cost parity

Indic users pay 4–8 tokens/word vs 1.4 for English — 3–5× the cost, latency, and less usable context. Closing the fertility gap is the biggest equity lever we hold.

Verifiable capability

Coding, math, and agency are bought with RLVR against strong verifiers. The verifier is the ceiling — so we build Indic verifiers, not just Indic data.

Model spec: 40B dense decoder (Gemma-3 lineage: GQA, 5:1 local/global attention, RoPE, RMSNorm, 128k context), tied embeddings, 256k vocab, ~16T pre-training tokens (deliberately over-trained ~20× past Chinchilla for cheap inference at Indian scale).

Every number below — vocab size, fertility targets, mix shares, filter thresholds — is first validated by ablation on small proxy models before it governs a trillion-token run: a recipe change should rank against alternatives, not follow intuition.

01Languages & scope

We do not pretend to serve 22 languages uniformly — digital-corpus depth falls off a cliff after the top ten, so we tier by (speaker base × available high-quality tokens) and budget capacity honestly.

Tier Languages Why
T1 — deep Hindi, English Hindi = largest base; English = India's link language and the substrate for code, science, and world knowledge — and the bar for Gemma-4 parity.
T2 — strong Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia High-population scheduled languages with usable (if thin) corpora. The Dravidian four are agglutinative → tokenizer-hard (see §2).
T3 — functional Assamese, Urdu, Sanskrit, Nepali, Maithili + Romanized / Hinglish Sanskrit for cultural + morphological signal that aids cross-Indic transfer; Romanized code-mixing is a first-class target — it is how Indians actually type.

~11 scripts total: Devanagari, Bengali-Assamese, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Odia, Perso-Arabic, Latin.

02Tokenizer: fertility targets → vocab sizecore

Fertility (subword tokens per word) is the load-bearing number: it sets inference cost, effective context, and training efficiency (high fertility burns the token budget re-encoding the same text). We set explicit per-bucket targets and derive vocab size to hit them.

Measurement: we track tokens/word and bytes/token. Dravidian agglutination makes a "word" long, so tokens/word runs high there; bytes/token normalizes across morphology and is the fairer cross-language target.

Code, math & science ride the Latin bucket at 1.35 tok/word — same script; digit-splitting and code-friendly merges keep identifiers, indentation, and LaTeX intact. Agentic work is not a fertility target but a control-token budget: ~1.5k reserved tokens so tool-call, function-name, and reasoning delimiters never fragment into subwords.

Why 256k specifically

  • Parity target: Gemma 3 ships 256k; Sarvam-30B ships 262k and is a proven Indic point. Matching this is the floor for Gemma-4-class multilingual coverage.
  • Scaling law: larger models deserve larger vocabularies (Tao et al., 2024) — Llama2-70B's compute-optimal vocab was ~216k even English-only. A 40B model with 11 scripts comfortably supports 256k.
  • Affordable: tied embeddings at 256k × ~6144 hidden ≈ 1.6B params ≈ 4% of 40B — standard, not a tax.

Construction details that actually move Indic fertility

  • Grapheme-cluster / matra-aware pre-tokenization. Naive BPE splits Devanagari conjuncts and combining vowel signs (matras) badly — the #1 cause of inflated Indic fertility.
  • Script-aware merges (never merge across scripts) + NFC + Indic Unicode normalization (nukta, ZWJ/ZWNJ) — without it, dedup and tokenization both fracture.
  • Digit-splitting (single digits or 3-digit groups) to help arithmetic.
  • Balanced training corpus: fit the tokenizer on an Indic-up-sampled mix so merges actually form on Indic — fit BPE on the natural English-dominated distribution and Indic fertility stays broken regardless of vocab size.
  • ~1.5k reserved special tokens for the agentic surface: tool-call boundaries, a function-name namespace, reasoning delimiters, role markers, code fences.

03Data: what we collect & why

Pre-training (~16T tokens)

The defining constraint: all clean Indic text in existence (AI4Bharat Sangraha) is ~251B tokens — and treat that as an upper bound, not a floor: Sangraha's own verified-human-origin split runs far below its headline total (e.g. Telugu falls from 16.3B to 3.7B, Odia from 12.5B to 1.2B, once machine-translated text is excluded). Yet 22% of 16T is ~3.5T. Verified Sangraha up-sampled ≤4 epochs yields ~1T; OCR (books/PDFs) and ASR (audio/AIR archives) add a bounded slice of India's non-web knowledge; a DeepSeekMath-style mining loop — a classifier seeded on verified Indic text mines more Indic pages out of raw CC at scale, the same seed-to-120B-tokens pattern used for math — extends this further; capped synthesis fills the residue. If the total still falls short of 3.5T, we lower the Indic share rather than trade quality for it: availability sets the ceiling, not the target.

Source Share What & why
Filtered English web ~38% FineWeb-Edu / DCLM / Nemotron-CC-style curation — the reasoning, science, and world-knowledge backbone.
Code ~22% The Stack v2 (900B unique, 600+ langs) + GitHub issues/PRs/notebooks + execution-verified synthetic. Over-weighted — code transfers strongly to reasoning and agency, but excess share measurably regresses non-code performance, so 22% is capped and watched by the general/coding guardrails in §5.
Indic (all sources) ~22% Sangraha + CC filtered by robust lang-ID + OCR of books/PDFs + ASR of audio/podcasts/AIR archives + filtered translation/synthesis. Up-sampled ≤4 epochs. OCR/ASR unlock India's knowledge that lives in print and speech, not on the web.
↳ of which India-first knowledge carved NCERT, Constitution + BNS/BNSS/BSA + judgments, UPSC/JEE/NEET prep, Indian history/geography/civics from Indian sources, public-domain Indic literature, Indian news, agriculture/Ayurveda. This corpus makes the defaults Indian.
Math & science ~8% proof-pile-2, arXiv, textbooks, StackExchange, synthetic step-by-step.
Non-Indic multilingual ~3% Small slice for cross-lingual transfer that lifts low-resource Indic.
Long-context / structured ~7% Books, long legal/scientific docs, tables/JSON for 128k context and tool-output formats.

Batching, not just filtering: the mix above is a target, not a promise a quality selector keeps on its own — English-tuned scoring systematically undervalues Brahmic-script and code-mixed text even with per-script classifiers. Indic and India-first data instead ride a fixed always-on floor (≈8% of every batch, independent of any selector's relative scoring), while a small golden-proxy slice of benchmark-adjacent text sets the classifiers' target direction and never itself enters a training batch.

Synthetic guardrail: synthetic Indic bridges the quality gap but is capped and filtered for translationese and model-collapse; factuality is checked by round-trip translation and teacher-consistency.

Post-training (SFT)

~1–2M curated samples, ≥40% Indic / India-first — deliberately above the 22% pre-training share, because post-training is where defaults and behavior are set; native-authored over translated wherever possible. Spanning:

  • Agentic trajectories — function calling, code execution, file/web/terminal tools, multi-step — including tasks instructed in Hindi/Tamil, so agency isn't English-only.
  • Coding — repo-level multi-file edits, bug-fix diffs, TDD, code review, with code-mixed comments.
  • Reasoning — math/logic chain-of-thought, authored in Indic as well as English.
  • Indic instruction — native-authored > pure translation (translationese kills naturalness): IndicAlign-Instruct + crowdsourced native data + filtered self-instruct.
  • Cultural / context — Indian etiquette, entities, units, and Hinglish code-switching.

RL / alignment

  • RLVR + GRPO (verl): code (unit tests/execution), math (answer checkers), agentic (sandboxed task success) — the SOTA lever for coding and agency.
  • Agentic RL in real tool/code/browser environments with outcome rewards and multi-turn credit assignment; SWE-RM-style reward models where execution isn't available.
  • RLHF/RLAIF for helpfulness and style, with Indic preference data (what makes a good Hindi answer, not a translated English one).
  • India-aware safety: a taxonomy for communal harmony, electoral integrity in Indian elections, religious/caste sensitivity, Indian legal compliance, and India-context misinformation — not a re-skinned Western safety set.

04Data cleaning, mapped to objectives

The recurring theme: English pipelines do not transfer to Indic. Classifiers, thresholds, PII patterns, and even Unicode need Indic-specific treatment — and aggressive filters tuned on abundant English silently delete scarce Indic.

Provenance & licence are captured at ingestion, before merge/shuffle/sharding makes them unrecoverable: source, transformations, and licence class (permissive / attribution / share-alike / unknown) travel with every document. One unknown-provenance source is enough to flag a mixture unusable, so such sources are excluded at collection, not filtered out later.

# Stage Indic / objective-specific treatment
1 Extraction trafilatura/resiliparse + Indic OCR (Surya) + ASR (IndicWav2Vec/Whisper) to convert print & audio into tokens.
2 Language ID IndicLID (CC mislabels Indic heavily); detect 22 languages + Romanized/code-mixed explicitly.
3 Normalization NFC + Indic canonicalization (nukta, ZWJ/ZWNJ) — prerequisite for correct dedup and tokenization.
4 Dedup MinHash-LSH near-dup + exact-substring; cross-lingual dedup so translations don't create hidden duplicates that fake extra epochs.
5 Quality filter English/code: FineWeb-Edu classifier + DCLM fastText + heuristics. Indic: train per-script educational-value classifiers (seeded by a strong multilingual teacher) — English classifiers don't transfer — plus Nemotron-CC-style ensembling with synthetic rephrasing. Per-language thresholds: up-weight quality rather than delete scarce data.
6 Safety / toxicity IndicAlign-Toxic + multilingual communal-slur filters; remove illegal/CSAM.
7 PII StarPII + Indic patterns (Aadhaar, PAN, phone).
8 Code-specific Permissive-license only, malicious-code removal, near-dedup, compilability/syntax filters, drop minified/autogenerated; keep tests & docstrings.
9 Synthetic hygiene Translationese detection, round-trip/teacher-consistency factuality checks, model-collapse monitoring.
10 Decontamination n-gram overlap removal against every eval set (MILU, IndicGenBench, SWE-bench, MMLU, GSM8K…).

05Evaluation: objective → benchmark

Two methodology rules dominate: report per-language, never just averages (averages hide low-resource collapse), and prefer execution/verifiable and native-human judgment over MCQ proxies wherever the objective is generation or agency.

Objective Benchmarks Bar / note
Indic understanding & knowledge MILU (11 langs), IndicMMLU-Pro, ParamBench, IndicXTREME Beat GPT-4o's 72% MILU average; track the weakest language, not the mean.
Indic generation IndicGenBench (29 langs: MT, summarization, QA) + native-human fluency panel Automated metrics miss translationese → human raters for the long tail.
India-first / cultural custom Bharat-Eval (we author it) Indian civics/law/history/geo/current-affairs from Indian sources; Indian-context defaults ("national anthem" → Indian), lakh/crore, festivals, code-switching. Fills a real benchmark gap.
Coding SWE-bench Verified, LiveCodeBench, HumanEval+/MBPP+, Indic-comment code Execution-based; agentic repo fixes are the headline.
Agentic SWE-bench Verified, terminal-bench, τ-bench, BFCL, WebArena + Indic-instructed tasks Multi-turn, tool-grounded, verifiable outcomes.
Math & science GSM8K, MATH, AIME, GPQA + Indic-translated math Check reasoning holds cross-lingually, not just in English.
General / long-context / IF MMLU-Pro, IFEval, RULER (128k), Arena-style Also serve as regression guardrails against Indic over-indexing.

Safety Indian-context safety (communal/electoral/legal), cross-Indic toxicity, jailbreak Evaluated in every T1/T2 language, not English-only.

06Training curriculum

Stage Tokens Focus
S1 · Pre-train ~14T Broad mix, English/code-heavy early to build the reasoning substrate.
S2 · Mid-train / anneal ~2T LR decay; up-weight high-quality Indic + India-first + math/code, extend context to 128k. The SOTA lever: the scarcest, best data pays off most when spent late.
S3 · SFT — Instruction + agentic + coding + reasoning + native Indic.
S4 · RL — RLVR (code/math/agentic) + RLHF (helpfulness, India-aware safety, Indic preference).

07Key bets & risks

Bet / risk Position & mitigation
Bet: over-trained 40B + low fertility = cheap, low-latency serving at Indian scale. The whole economic thesis, chosen over Chinchilla-optimal loss.
Risk: Indic data scarcity (~2.5T gap) Budgeted in §3 (OCR/ASR, then a DeepSeekMath-style mining loop, then capped synthesis); residual lever = lower the Indic share before the quality bar. Watch collapse & translationese.
Risk: Indic over-indexing regresses English/global Guardrail evals (MMLU/coding) + capped Indic epochs + cross-lingual transfer slice.
Risk: 256k vocab under-trains rare tokens Tie embeddings, up-sample tokenizer corpus toward Indic, monitor rare-token gradient norms.
Risk: weak Indic verifiers cap RL Build Indic math/factuality/code verifiers before scaling RL — the verifier is the ceiling.

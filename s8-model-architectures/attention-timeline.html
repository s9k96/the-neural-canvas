The Field Changes Its Mind

Vanilla attention was never wrong. It was expensive, and every mechanism since is somebody looking at that bill and trying to pay less of it. Laid out by the date each one actually appeared — not by family, not by teaching order — you can watch the field's priorities move: exactness, then decode memory, then length, then recurrent state coming back, then sparsity coming back, then compression getting aggressive. Every mechanism is drawn from its own rule — the grids below run the real selection function rather than illustrating it — and every date was checked against the paper's v1 record, not recalled.

Scores

Two facts that generate the rest of this page

Every token is compared with every other token. Six tokens give 36 scores. Ten thousand give one hundred million. And attention itself does not know order — the same word in two places projects to the same query, key and value. Those two facts are the compute bill and the position problem, and between them they account for every mechanism below.

02The bill, calculated

The two costs arrive at different moments. Compute lands during training and prefill and grows as T². KV cache lands during generation, grows as T, and is private to each conversation — model weights load once and are shared, but eight users means eight caches.

Yardstick from §10 of the class notes: 48 layers, head dim 128, bf16. 2 × layers × kv_heads × head_dim × T × batch × bytes. At 8 KV heads and 32,768 tokens this page computes 6.44 GB for one user and 51.54 GB for eight — the notes' own published figures, reproduced rather than copied.

04Every mechanism, and what it costs you

Each card answers three questions: what it buys, what it gives up, and when you would actually pick it. A mechanism that is right for a 2K chatbot and wrong for a 1M agent is not a bad mechanism — so set the workload and the verdicts move.

05Where the dates came from

The assignment warns that an agent will invent a launch date and describe a half-remembered technique. So none of these are recalled. Every arXiv entry below was re-fetched and its <published> field — the v1 submission timestamp — compared against the date this page displays.

Run python verify_sources.py and it exits non-zero if any date has moved, if the chronology is out of order, or if a card on this page disagrees with the evidence file. The three claims that cannot be machine-checked are listed as exempt with the reason, never silently skipped.

What this page does not claim. The cost model is the KV-cache formula from §10 applied to one fixed reference shape, so it compares mechanisms against each other on equal terms — it does not predict any real deployment's memory, which also pays for weights, activations, attention workspaces and allocator headroom. Nothing here measures quality: every quality statement on a card is attributed to its paper's own reported results, not re-measured. And a reported extension factor or speedup is evidence for that model and that procedure, not a promise that it transfers.
